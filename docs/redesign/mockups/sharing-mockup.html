Detecting vs. matching: an unusable id is always detectable — either it's missing from the catalog or it isn't offered for that piece. Suggesting a replacement is the part that needs the color to still exist. A re-mixed color is the one true blind spot: it applies under its id, which is intended since the catalog is the source of truth. Future: a paid off-palette request rides as piece=~ff00ff.
